# Brag Plan: SupplyChain Nexus

## What is this app?
SupplyChain Nexus is a multi-agent resilience control tower that traces supplier disruptions through a manufacturing network, simulates recovery strategies, and routes the chosen plan to human approval.

## The angle
Treat the disruption like a live operational incident, not a feature tour. The first frame hits with the concrete problem: fourteen days, three plants, one hundred twenty-seven orders. The system then moves from red exposure to green action, ending on the approval gate that makes the AI useful without making it reckless.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dark operations canvas snaps on. Three large figures lock in one after another: `14 DAYS`, `3 PLANTS`, `127 ORDERS`. The disruption label appears last: `SUP-042 / CRITICAL`.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The actual control-tower language: `$4.20M revenue exposure`, `5.2 days runway`, and the topology from supplier to plants.
- Three recovery options step into the frame, then resolve into `ALTERNATE SUPPLIER + BUFFER TRANSFER` with `$4.12M PROTECTED`.
- The Recovery Command approval gate lands as the trust moment: `$192K authorization required`, `HUMAN APPROVAL PENDING`.

## Outro / punchline
The red network settles into a calm green status line: `SEE THE DISRUPTION. SIMULATE THE FUTURE. ORCHESTRATE THE RESPONSE.` Then the SupplyChain Nexus mark locks in with `FOUNDRY-READY RESILIENCE OPERATIONS`.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: a critical supplier disruption appears in the control tower.  
Key action: the operator investigates the cascade and compares recovery strategies.  
Result: a grounded recovery plan waits for an authorized human decision.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with polished operational accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: low under narration, gentle lift during the recovery reveal, fade under the final lockup
- Music cue guidance: use the bundled cue preset `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; lock the main recovery reveal to the nearest strong cue and keep sequential data reveals on a readable every-other-beat rhythm
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the blue background glow breathe with RMS energy, never use a waveform
- SFX posture: sparse and precise; one UI click for the incident lock, one soft impact for the protected-value reveal, one clean confirmation for the approval gate
- Audio-coupled moments: sequential hook figures, topology nodes, strategy rows, final approval status
- Restraint rule: narration must remain intelligible; SFX never compete with the voice

## Voiceover script
Fourteen days. Three plants. One hundred twenty-seven customer orders at risk. SupplyChain Nexus traces the disruption through the digital twin, simulates the recovery options, and turns the best move into an approval-ready plan. Every number is grounded. Every action is accountable. See the disruption. Simulate the future. Orchestrate the response.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Critical event — 3.2s
A red incident rail slices across a dark operations canvas. `SUP-042 / CRITICAL` locks in, followed by `14 DAYS`, `3 PLANTS`, and `127 ORDERS`. Use the source app's disruption language and rose alert color.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the three figures arrive in order and then hold together for readability.
Audio intent: immediate urgency, not panic.
Audio-coupled idea: a restrained click on the incident lock and low-impact hit on the final figure.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Cascade — 4.1s
The control-tower network topology assembles: supplier node, red paths, Stuttgart, Detroit, Yokohama, and the Munich buffer. A large side panel shows `$4.20M REVENUE EXPOSURE` and `5.2 DAYS RUNWAY`.
Sequential/interaction: yes — topology nodes and routes appear one by one; labels remain settled after arrival.
Audio intent: analytical momentum.
Audio-coupled idea: soft drops on the first and last node, no sound on every label.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Simulate — 4.2s
Three strategy rows slide in as real operational choices: `EXPEDITE`, `REALLOCATE`, `ALTERNATE SUPPLIER`. A blue selection line resolves on `ALTERNATE SUPPLIER + BUFFER TRANSFER`; the right panel counts up to `$4.12M PROTECTED`.
Sequential/interaction: yes — strategy rows enter top-to-bottom, then the recommendation locks.
Audio intent: confidence builds through comparison.
Audio-coupled idea: card-place accents on the first and final row, soft impact on the recommendation.
Transition mood: velocity-matched slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Human gate — 4.0s
Recovery Command appears as a focused approval panel. Show `$192K AUTHORIZATION REQUIRED`, `4 EXECUTABLE ACTIONS`, `POLICY GROUNDED`, and a clear `HUMAN APPROVAL PENDING` status. A cursor-like blue line moves to the approval control but stops before execution.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the financial threshold, policy grounding, and approval status reveal in that order.
Audio intent: trust and restraint.
Audio-coupled idea: quiet click on the threshold, clean confirmation tone on the pending state.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Lockup — 4.0s
The red route lines resolve to emerald. The headline locks in: `SEE THE DISRUPTION.` Then `SIMULATE THE FUTURE.` Then `ORCHESTRATE THE RESPONSE.` SupplyChain Nexus sits below with `FOUNDRY-READY RESILIENCE OPERATIONS`.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three short lines appear with enough hold to read, then the brand mark lands.
Audio intent: composed finish and memorability.
Audio-coupled idea: one understated confirmation accent on the final brand lockup.
Transition mood: clean hold → end
